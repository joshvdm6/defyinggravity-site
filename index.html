Defying Gravity

A video journal for calisthenics skills. Log short attempts at your planche, front lever or handstand, then watch months of progress on a timeline, a calendar and a side-by-side compare.

Your videos stay on your phone — the app never uploads them, and never changes the originals in your camera roll.
